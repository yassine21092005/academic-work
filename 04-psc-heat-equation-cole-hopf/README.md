# PSC (second-year collective scientific project): Heat equation and the Cole-Hopf transform

Ongoing team project at École polytechnique (team of five, supervised by tutors), 2026-2027.

- `psc_detailed_proposal.pdf`: detailed project proposal (MAP 11, September 2026, in French).

**Subject.** Power-series (Taylor) expansion in space of the heat equation with exponential-polynomial terminal condition g(x) = exp(P(x)), and of its Cole-Hopf logarithmic transform. The expansion turns the PDE into an infinite system of coupled ODEs; the goal is to find and justify a closure condition that selects the Feynman-Kac solution, and to quantify convergence.

**Link with quantitative finance.** In polynomial volatility models, the characteristic function of the log-price is an expectation of exponentials of polynomials, the same kind of object. Once it is computed, Lewis' formula gives call and put prices by Fourier transform, as a faster alternative to Monte Carlo when no closed form exists.

The subject was proposed by E. Abi Jaber and E. Attal (École polytechnique). Code and results are kept in the team's shared repository; this folder only contains the proposal.
