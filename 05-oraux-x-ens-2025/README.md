# Oraux X-ENS MP 2025: feedback book for the X and ENS oral exams

Collective book (189 pages, in French) written with fellow students from the Lydex preparatory classes (Akram El Omrani, Mohamed Amine Khalil, Adam Bennaceur), as a continuation of the 2022, 2023 and 2024 editions.

**Content**
- About one hundred exercises actually asked at the X and ENS oral exams in 2025, with a detailed account of how each oral went and comments written by the candidates.
- Detailed proposed solutions; my own part was mainly correcting the mathematics orals, working in a team on collection, structuring, writing and proofreading.
- Feedback from about thirty candidates of the 2025 session.

The book is not hosted in this repository. Download link (from the LinkedIn post): https://lnkd.in/e-pRnYYv
